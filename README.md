# Linde-EMEA-Sales-Analytics-Dashboard

This project was developed as part of a Business Intelligence case study for a Data Analytics role.
The objective was to analyze historical sales transactions from a gas cylinder distributor and transform raw transactional data into actionable business insights for executive stakeholders.
The solution was designed to answer key business questions regarding sales performance, customer behavior, product demand, and sales channel effectiveness through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

# Project Overview

Linde EMEA Sales Analytics Dashboard is an end-to-end Business Intelligence project developed from a real interview case study. 
Using over 224,000 transactional sales records, the project demonstrates how raw operational data can be transformed into executive-level insights through data modeling, DAX, and interactive Power BI dashboards. The analysis focuses on sales performance, customer behavior, product demand, channel effectiveness, and customer retention, concluding with data-driven business recommendations for executive stakeholders.

# Business Problem

The company provided six years of transactional sales data (2017–2022) for a single European market.
The goal was to support executive decision-making by answering questions such as:
How has the business performed over time?
Which products generate the highest revenue?
Which customer segments drive sales?
Which sales channels perform best?
How has the customer base evolved?
What opportunities exist to improve business performance?

# Dataset

The dataset contains approximately:
224,000+ order line transactions
165,000+ customer orders
7,200+ customers
Sales history from 2017–2022
Each record represents an order line, meaning a single order may contain multiple products.

# Data Preparation

Before building the dashboard, the data was validated and transformed by:
Understanding the dataset granularity (order-line level).
Validating data types.
Checking duplicate order lines.
Verifying quantity and currency units.
Creating a Calendar table.
Building relationships following a star schema approach.

# Key Business Findings

The analysis identified several important business insights:
Revenue Decline
Revenue remained stable between 2017 and 2019 before declining steadily through 2022.

Customer Base Reduction
The number of active customers decreased over time, suggesting customer retention is a key business challenge.

Revenue Concentration
Revenue is concentrated among a relatively small number of customers and product groups.

Strong Digital Channel
E-Commerce is the company's highest-performing sales channel.

Industry Dependence
Construction is the largest customer segment, representing a significant share of total revenue.

# Business Recommendations
-Based on the analysis, several strategic recommendations were proposed:
-Improve customer retention through targeted loyalty initiatives.
-Continue investing in digital sales channels.
-Protect key accounts through strategic account management.
-Diversify the customer portfolio to reduce dependency on a single industry.
-Investigate the drivers behind the revenue decline after 2019.
